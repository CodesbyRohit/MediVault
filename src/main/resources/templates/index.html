Why MediVault

  • One organised place

    Add, edit and search your records by title, type, date and provider, and attach the PDF reports.

  • Private by default

    Every record belongs to one account. Other patients and administrators cannot open it.

  • Secure sign-in

    Passwords are stored only as strong one-way hashes, and every form is protected against forgery.

How it works

  1. Create an account with your name, email and a password.
  2. Log in to reach your personal dashboard.
  3. Add your records and attach PDF reports. Only you can see or download them.
